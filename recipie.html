New York Cheesecake

Ingredients

For the base:

  • 3.5 oz (100 grams) butter cold, cut into cubes
  • ¼ cup (50 grams) sugar
    • Natural sugar replacement such as Swerve can be used for people with diabates
  • 1 cup (140 grams) flour
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
    • Vanilla flovaoured almond,oat or soy milk can also be used
  • 2 egg yolks

For the Filling:

  • 3.3 lbs (1½ kilograms) cream cheese
    • Greek yogurt,Hummus can also be used
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1½ tsp grated lemon peel
  • 1 tsp grated orange peel
  • ⅓ cup (75 ml) sweet cream
  • ⅓ cup (46 grams) flour
  • 4 whole eggs
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 8 oz (225 grams) sugar

Preparation steps:

  1. The day before preparing the cake,place the cheese in a dense strainer,and the refrigereator and drain the cheese from liquid
  2. pulse butter and pour in a good processor until you get a mixture of coarse crumbs, Add the vanilla an egg yolks and process briefly just until you get a lump of dough
  3. press the dough into bottom of pan with your hands,pierce with a fork and bake for 25 minutes in an oven preheated tp 302F degrees(150c) until the dough takes on a light golden color.Cool on a wire rack
  4. Increase the oven temperature to 375F degrees
  5. mix cheese with vanilla and zest until a uniform mixture is obtained .Add cream and mix until just combined,Finally gradually add flour.
  6. Beat the eggs and yolks with the sugar at medium-high speed for 8 to 10minutes until the mixture is puffy and pale.Fold the egg mixture into cheese mixture in 3 batches.
  7. Transfer to a pan with your pre-baked base and place on a baling sheet.Bake for about 11 minutes.The cake will rise a little,should not brown and remain light.Immediately lower the temperature to 212F degrees.Bake for another 49 minutes or more,until the top looks bright and dry,the edges are firm and the center vibrates a little
  8. Turn off the oven,open the door a little and leave the cake inside until completely cool,Run a small knife around the edges from the sides,cover and place in the refrigerator overnight
  9. Remove from the refrigerator about 20 minutes minutes before serving and slice with a knife dipped in hot water and cleaned
