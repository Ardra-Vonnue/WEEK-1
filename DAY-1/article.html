Gujarat Alkalies & Chemicals to set up Rs. 67 crore high-purity hydrogen peroxide plant

Gujarat Alkalies and Chemicals Limited has approved a major new investment to expand into high-end speciality chemicals used in advanced manufacturing sectors. In a regulatory filing, the company said its Board of Directors,at a meting held on 29 May 2026,cleared the proposal to set up a 5,000 TPA 1 high purity grade Hydrogen Peroxide (H2O2) plant at Dahej,Gujarat,with an estimated outlay of Rs. 67 crore.

Gujarat is one among the few states within india which can offer a complete ecosystem offering that will ensure success of a prominent hi-tech chemical companiesss The company stated the project is aimed at serving fast-growing niche industries,particularly semiconductor fabrication,solar cell manufacturing and other advanced electronics applications

This will enable the Company to produce High Purity grade Hydrogen peroxide for the niche applications in semiconductor fabrication,Solar cell manufacturing and other advanced electronics applications for which manufacturing units are being put up in india the dahej plant is expected to address growing domestic demand from semiconductor fabrication facilities and solar cell manufacturers as india accelerates efforts tp strengthen its electronic manufacturing and clean energy ecosystems.

The Dahej facility is expected to support India's broader push towars self-reliance in critical materials required for high-tech production manufacturing while expanding GaCL's presence in value-added speciality chemicals As per The Hindu BusinessLine,GACL's move ref;ects growing demand for high-purity chemicals used in semiconductor and solar manufacturing.The project is projected tp generate annual revenue of nearly 42 crores once operational.The company will fund the investment primarily through accurals,while any funding gap met through borrowings.
